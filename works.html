Signature Work

In The Pocket

An award-winning, interdisciplinary one-woman show by Hillary-Marie

Trailer coming soon

In The Pocket transforms the void of silence into a world brimming with music, movement, and magic.

Weaving together foot, body, and vocal percussion to turn both stage and audience into a living instrument, Hillary-Marie shares her story, beginning with the drum lessons her father gave her. And from there, she traces how she found tap dance, threading in the history and oral tradition of the art form.

Celebrated for its fusion of choreography, storytelling, live improvisation, and comedic timing, In The Pocket invites audiences into rhythm. Not just to hear it, but to feel it, breathe it, and experience it together.

Developed during her 2019 to 2022 fellowship at NJPAC.

At a glance

  • Solo show for theaters, festivals and schools
  • Run time: 45 minutes
  • Tech: sprung wooden tap dance floor (min. 6’ x 4’), 2 floor mics, 1 wireless headset mic, 2 monitors

Educator

Hillary-Marie is widely regarded as a teacher of teachers. For nearly two decades she has taught tap dancers and dance educators across four continents, pairing the craft of the art form with its history and oral tradition.

Her teaching is built on two frameworks she developed: The 5 Fundamentals of Tap Dance and The Fundamental Fusion Approach.

At a glance

  • Founder of iTapOnline.com (since 2016), educating more than 350 tap dancers and tap teachers monthly
  • Creator of the iTap Teacher Certificate Program, training hundreds of educators annually
  • Founder of Jersey Tap Fest (2010–2020)
  • Director, FutureSTEP Tap Company (2016–2023)
  • Co-Founder of Grooves Unlimited Dance Studio (2012–2023)

Signature classes

Rhythm Remix

Footwork, flow, and feel. This class remixes rhythms in real time, building fluid movement and a deeper connection to the music.

Body, Voice, Feet: One Instrument

Footwork, vocal rhythm and movement come together as one connected instrument. A playful way to experience music from head to toe.

Improve Your Improv

Playful exercises using body percussion, voice and feet build confidence, listening skills and the joy of creating in the moment.
